TAPEDECK

Your home screen is a turntable. Your apps are a crate of tapes.

Turntable Stage Vinyl Interface

Play/pause, scratch, and browse with tactile vinyl turntable gestures. Record spins only when music plays. Empty state shows "NO SIGNAL" when no session.

Crate Browser App Organization

Manual and smart crates (recent, music, comms, dev, games). Drag to reorder, fling to shuffle. Cards show app identity — no fake previews.

Quick TTY Terminal Drawer

Swipe down from the deck for a compact terminal without leaving. Full-screen TUIs, packages and colours work via Termux loopback SSH.

Now Playing Music Hero

Your current track is the focal point. Notification-listener metadata for Spotify, YouTube Music. Transport controls: prev, play, next, repeat.

Local-Only Privacy First

No accounts, no cloud, no telemetry. Terminal traffic is loopback SSH to Termux on the same device only.
